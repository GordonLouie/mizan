## ⭐ Best path: ask Chong for a direct introduction first
These are very senior people (MPFA MD, HKMA Deputy CE). A **forwarded introduction
from Prof. Chong** (he emails them and CCs you) gets a reply far more reliably than
a cold note from a stranger. Since he already offered, consider replying to him:
*"Would you be comfortable making a quick email introduction to Mr Cheng and Mr Lee?
A line from you would carry far more weight than a cold note — I'll keep it brief
from there."* Use the cold versions below only if a direct intro isn't possible.
