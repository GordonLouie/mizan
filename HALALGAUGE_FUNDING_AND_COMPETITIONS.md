# HalalGauge — Funding & Competitions Kit
*Accelerator / competition application kit. Tailored angles for HASAN.VC, NextIF, and JUMPSTARTER, a reusable pitch narrative, and the Southeast-Asia coverage-expansion angle. Verify every eligibility caveat before applying — these programmes change terms and windows.*

---

## Why apply at all

You're solo, pre-revenue, and need three things these programmes give better than raw fundraising: **credibility** (a cohort logo is a trust signal you badly need given the scholar gap), **network/intros** (including — critically — routes to finance-qualified scholars and design partners), and **non-dilutive or light capital** to extend runway. Money is the least important reason. Optimise applications for the intro and credibility value.

**Honest framing to keep:** never claim certification or scholarly endorsement you don't have. "Transparent, multi-standard, cited-source screener; building formal Shariah oversight" is your true and compelling story. Lead with it.

---

## Reusable pitch narrative (adapt per programme)

**One-liner:**
> HalalGauge is a multi-scholar halal investing engine that lets Muslims screen investments under the exact scholarly standard they follow (AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani, Dow Jones Islamic, Ja'fari, strict, or custom) and shows the precise financial ratios and cited primary source behind every verdict — no black box.

**The problem:** Existing halal screeners are black boxes. They give one "halal / not halal" verdict with no reasoning, no source, and no acknowledgement that scholars genuinely disagree. Muslim investors are asked to trust an opaque score across a $2T+ Islamic-finance market. **(Verify the market-size figure before using it in a deck.)**

**The insight:** Halal screening is not one answer — it's a *choice of standard*. HalalGauge is the first tool built around that truth: pick your standard, see the math, see the source.

**What's live (be exact, don't inflate):** live beta, ~650 stocks, free. Features: multi-standard screening with cited sources, portfolio compliance tracking, a "drift watch" that flags holdings sliding toward non-compliance *before* earnings, a purification calculator, and a Halal Asset Map covering 63 asset classes.

**The wedge:** transparency + multi-standard choice is a strong retail differentiator. We become the *cited authority* — the reference people point to — then extend into ETFs, REITs, funds, and (later) crypto.

**Founder:** solo technical founder, Hong Kong-based, medical-student background — built the whole product. (Frame the med-student angle as discipline/rigour + unusual founder, not as a liability.)

**The honest gap + the plan:** no formal Shariah board *yet* — building it via an academic relationship (Prof. James Frankel, CUHK) leading toward an intro to a finance-qualified scholar and a named advisor. Stated plainly because transparency is the whole brand.

**Why now (HK/Asia tailwind):** HK is positioning as a North Asia Islamic-finance hub channelling GCC capital into Asia; Southeast Asia (Malaysia, Indonesia) is the world's largest Muslim retail-investing base. **(Verify HK-hub specifics before citing.)**

**The ask:** [tailor to programme] — capital, mentorship, and specifically intros to (a) finance-qualified Shariah scholars and (b) a design partner (halal fintech / educator / community org).

## The Southeast-Asia coverage-expansion angle (use in ALL three)

This is your growth story and it maps directly onto these programmes' geographies. Make it concrete:

- **The market is SE Asia.** Malaysia and Indonesia are the deepest halal retail-investing populations on earth, with active Islamic capital markets and regulators (Malaysia's Securities Commission publishes Shariah-compliant securities lists; Indonesia has its own screening regime). **Verify current specifics before citing.**
- **The expansion move:** add coverage of **Bursa Malaysia and IDX (Indonesia) equities**, and screen them under both global standards (AAOIFI/DJIM) *and* local reference lists — then show users where the standards agree and disagree. That "local + global, side by side, with sources" view is something the black-box incumbents don't offer.
- **Why it's credible for you:** it reuses your existing ratio engine and cited-source model — it's a data-and-coverage expansion, not a new product. Ties directly to Roadmap Track C (coverage) and Track A (standard parity).
- **Why accelerators love it:** it's a clear, geographically-relevant growth thesis with a large addressable retail base, and it gives them a reason their region/network is the right home for you.

## Programme-by-programme application angles

### 1. HASAN.VC — halal-startup accelerator (SE-Asia focus, ~US$60k, a cohort's applications open)
- **Angle:** You are *exactly* their thesis — a halal-focused startup with a direct SE-Asia growth path. Lead hard on the **SE-Asia coverage-expansion angle** above: "we're built for the world's largest halal retail-investing market and want to expand coverage into Malaysia/Indonesia — your network is where that happens."
- **Emphasise:** the halal mission is core, not bolted on; transparency as the differentiator against black-box screeners; a solo technical founder who ships.
- **The ask:** capital + SE-Asia market intros + (crucially) intros to finance-qualified scholars and a regional design partner.
- **Handle the scholar gap head-on:** in a halal-focused programme, they *will* ask. Pre-empt it: "no formal board yet; here's the concrete path." Honesty scores here.
- **Verify:** cohort dates and whether applications are currently open; the ~US$60k figure and its terms (equity %, program length, location/travel expectations); eligibility for a solo, HK-incorporated founder targeting SE Asia.

### 2. NextIF — Islamic-finance accelerator (free)
- **Angle:** Most Islamic-finance-native audience of the three. Go deep on **methodology rigour**: the multi-standard implementation, cited primary sources, the standard-vs-madhhab distinction you handle carefully, purification/khums treatment. This audience will respect the depth and the honesty about what you don't yet have.
- **Emphasise:** you're building the transparent reference layer for Islamic investing; the drift-watch and purification features that show genuine domain understanding.
- **The ask:** since it's free, the value is network and credibility — explicitly ask for scholar intros and Islamic-finance-industry connections. This programme may be your single best route to a finance-qualified scholar.
- **Verify:** that it's genuinely free with no equity; current cohort timing; eligibility (location, stage); what "accelerator" concretely includes (mentorship vs. capital vs. just network).

### 3. JUMPSTARTER — Alibaba Entrepreneurs Fund, Hong Kong
- **Angle:** This is a broad HK/mainland competition, **not** halal-specific. Do NOT over-index on the religious mission — frame it as a **fintech with a large, underserved, global vertical market and an Asia/HK expansion thesis**. Emphasise the HK base, the incorporation, the tech execution, and the SE-Asia (and GCC-into-Asia) growth story that fits HK's Islamic-finance-hub narrative.
- **Emphasise:** market size, defensibility (becoming the cited authority + coverage moat), solo-founder execution, and the HK-hub tailwind that makes this a *HK* story.
- **The ask:** exposure, capital/prize, and access to the Alibaba/HK ecosystem network.
- **Watch the framing:** a general-tech judge won't weight the scholar gap heavily, but don't hide it — a one-line honest mention is enough here.
- **Verify:** current application window; eligibility (stage, incorporation location — HK incorporation likely helps or is required); whether pre-revenue solo founders fit the profile they select.

- [ ] **Real usage/retention numbers** ready — instrument the product before applying (Roadmap Track D). "Pre-revenue" is fine; "no data at all" is weak.
- [ ] **The honest scholar-gap paragraph** written once, reused — never claim certification you lack.
- [ ] **The SE-Asia coverage-expansion slide** — your growth thesis, same story tailored per programme.
- [ ] **A 2-minute live demo** — screen one stock showing ratios + cited source + the side-by-side standards view. Your transparency is best *shown*, not told.
- [ ] **Founder story** framed as an asset (unusual, disciplined, ships alone).

## Eligibility caveats to verify (do not assume — these gate acceptance)
- **All three:** current application windows/cohort dates (may be open, closed, or rolling).
- **All three:** whether a **solo, pre-revenue, HK-incorporating** founder fits their stage/profile requirements.
- **HASAN.VC:** the ~US$60k amount, equity taken, program length, and any relocation/travel to SE Asia.
- **NextIF:** confirm it's genuinely free/no-equity and what's actually delivered.
- **JUMPSTARTER:** incorporation/geography requirements and whether the fit is truly fintech-general (it is not halal-specific).
- **Any market-size or HK-hub figures** used in decks — verify before citing publicly.

Apply to all three; they're cheap to enter relative to the upside. Rank the value as: **NextIF and HASAN.VC for scholar/partner intros and mission-fit credibility; JUMPSTARTER for HK-ecosystem exposure and capital.** Win the intro and the logo, and the money is a bonus. Whatever you write, it must be true — your entire brand is that you don't hide the ball.
