# Mizan — the auditable halal investing platform

**Invest with confidence it's halal — every verdict tied to a named scholar and a cited source.**
[mizaninvesting.com](https://mizaninvesting.com) · Hong Kong

---

### The opportunity
Global Islamic finance assets reached **~$5.2 trillion in 2025** and are projected to surpass
**$7.5 trillion by 2028** (Standard Chartered). Yet the ~2 billion Muslims worldwide — and the
institutions serving them — still lack trustworthy, transparent tools to know whether a given
investment is Shariah-compliant. Existing screeners pick a single standard and ask users to take
the verdict on faith. In Hong Kong specifically, there is **no Shariah-compliant MPF option**,
even as the city actively promotes itself as an Islamic-finance hub.

### What Mizan is
A halal stock-screening and analytics platform that tells investors not just *whether* a stock is
compliant, but *why* — across multiple scholarly standards, with the underlying numbers and
primary sources shown. Live today:

- **Multi-scholar verdicts** (AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani, Dow Jones Islamic, and more) with the
  exact ratios, thresholds, and **cited sources** behind each call.
- **Halal indexes** with real, point-in-time backtests vs the S&P 500.
- **Grounded AI compliance reports** for any stock or portfolio (the verdict and figures are
  computed deterministically; the AI only writes the analysis — never fabricated).
- **Portfolio compliance scoring** with halal-swap suggestions.
- An **institutional data API** for bulk screening and white-label use.

### Why Mizan is different
- **Transparency.** Competitors give a yes/no; Mizan shows the standard, the math, the source, and
  *where scholars disagree* — the trust layer a religiously-sensitive decision demands.
- **Data integrity by design.** Built for scrutiny: real data or clearly-labelled estimates,
  never fabricated returns. This is the bar institutions and regulators require.
- **Institutional-grade deliverables** retail apps lack: screened indexes, auditable AI reports,
  and a licensable data feed.

### Business model
- **Retail** — freemium; Pro at **$9.99/mo** (free verdicts drive acquisition via search).
- **Institutional** — data API, white-label compliance reports, and index licensing for funds,
  banks, and pension/MPF providers.
