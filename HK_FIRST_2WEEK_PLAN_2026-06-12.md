# Mizan — HK-first 2-week plan (online only)

Runs ~12 Jun → 26 Jun, ending right at your Day-30 exit gate (~27–28 Jun). This
is the final push before the decision. Goal isn't vanity traffic — it's to
manufacture the two signals the gate needs: **unprompted shares** and
**retention** (people coming back on their own).

## The core constraint → the core mechanic

You can't do in-person, so your local advantage has to run through **other
people's groups**. The whole plan rests on three online levers that don't need
you in a room:

1. **Community-org group forwards** — get HK Muslim orgs/MSAs to share Mizan into
   their member WhatsApp/IG groups (HKUSTSU already did this). This is your
   masjid substitute.
2. **A remote design-partner group** — 10–15 HK Muslim investors in one WhatsApp
   group you run. This is where retention and shares actually get observed.
3. **The academic trio** — Chong / Pawee / Frankel Zooms for credibility + a
   scholar introduction.

## Week 2 (19–26 Jun): convert, observe, read the signal

**Mon 19 – Wed 21**
- Run the design-partner group actively: post a prompt — *"screen a stock you
  actually own and tell me if the verdict surprised you."* Watch who engages,
  who returns, who forwards it to a friend. **This group is your live retention +
  share test.**
- Follow up on any org forwards: did anyone from those groups reach you? (A cold
  inbound from a forwarded group = your first true unprompted share.)
- Pawee Zoom whenever scheduled; same scholar-intro ask.

**Thu 22 (your Day-18-equivalent read)** — honest checkpoint:
- Did anyone return on their own / forward it unprompted? Did any org forward
  actually produce an inbound?
- **Green** (any unprompted shares + people returning): continue, and start the
  Cyberport track (below).
- **Yellow** (engagement but no shares): the HK-first community angle is your last
  best lever — push the design-partner group one more week.
- **Red** (orgs forwarded, group tried it, still zero return/zero share): that's a
  real demand signal, not a distribution one. Respect the gate.

**Fri 23 – Thu 26** — whichever way Thu 22 points: either deepen the design-partner
group + convert a scholar intro, or begin a graceful wind-down per your
pre-commitment.

## The one number that matters

Not visitors. **Unprompted shares + design-partner retention.** Everything above
is engineered to produce and measure those two things by the gate. If the
HK-first community push — your single best and most reachable channel — doesn't
move them in two weeks, that's the honest answer the Council pre-committed you to
hear.

## What stays off the list

No paid ads. No new features (the product's good enough to test). No spraying
global Reddit/X. No in-person (by your constraint). One market, one mechanic,
one decision at the end.
