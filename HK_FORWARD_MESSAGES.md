# HK-first forward messages — ready to send

Three messages: (1) ask an org to forward, (2) the blurb they paste into their
group, (3) recruit your design-partner group. Copy, send, log in the tracker.

---

## 1. The ask — DM to orgs/MSAs (HKIYA, Islamic Union, Muslim Council, the MSAs)

Send to the accounts you're already in contact with (warmest first):

> Assalamu alaikum, and thank you again. Would you be open to sharing Mizan with
> your members? I've written a short blurb below you can forward to your WhatsApp
> / IG group as-is — no edits needed. It's a free halal stock screener (no
> signup, no data collected) that shows a stock's verdict under all 6 scholars
> side by side, including Mufti Taqi Usmani's illiquid-asset rule that no other
> app implements. I'm genuinely just after honest feedback from people who'd
> actually use it. Jazakum Allah khair either way.

---

## 2. The forwardable blurb — what they paste into the group

Written so a member reading it gets the value in 10 seconds:

> Salam all — sharing a free tool worth a look: **mizaninvesting.com**, a halal
> stock screener. Unlike Zoya/Musaffa, it shows whether a stock is halal under
> *all 6 scholarly methodologies side by side* — including Mufti Taqi Usmani's
> illiquid-asset rule, which no other app implements — with every threshold
> cited to its source. No signup, no account, no data collected. If you invest,
> try it on a stock you own. It's built by a CUHK student (openly, he's not
> Muslim himself — he just wanted this transparency to exist) and he genuinely
> wants feedback: hello@mizaninvesting.com.

---

## 3. Design-partner group — recruit 10–15 testers

Send to individuals (via Miqdad's network, MSA members who replied, warm
contacts). The goal: a small WhatsApp group whose real usage + word-of-mouth is
your distribution test.

> Salam — I'm putting together a small group (10–15 people) to help shape Mizan,
> a free halal stock screener, over the next two weeks. Light commitment: try it
> on your own holdings, tell me what's confusing or missing, and react to a few
> updates. If you invest — even a little — and care about getting halal screening
> right, I'd love you in. Reply and I'll add you. I'm building in the open —
> Gordon, CUHK, not Muslim myself, which is exactly why I want a group keeping me
> honest.

---

## After sending
- Log each send in `Mizan_Outreach_Tracker.xlsx`.
- Watch for the real signal: if anyone from a forwarded group reaches *you* cold,
  that's your first true unprompted share — mark it.
- For the design-partner group: once it has ~8 people, post one prompt —
  "screen a stock you actually own and tell me if the verdict surprised you" —
  and watch who comes back. That's your retention read.
