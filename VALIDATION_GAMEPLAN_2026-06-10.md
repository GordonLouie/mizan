## The core question this sprint answers

Zero unprompted shares is consistent with two opposite truths:
- **(A) Demand is wrong** — the multi-scholar/transparency angle is a vitamin,
  not a painkiller; the mass market wants a trusted authority to simplify, not a
  comparison dashboard.
- **(B) Demand is fine, distribution failed** — a solo, part-time, non-Muslim
  founder ran weak distribution for 30 days on an until-now-broken product.

These demand opposite decisions (walk vs. one more focused push). Your single
"3 shares" metric can't tell them apart. **Retention can.** This sprint is built
to disambiguate them.
