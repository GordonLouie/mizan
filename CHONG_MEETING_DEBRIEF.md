## 0. The big shift (read this)
Chong's advice points to a **different business than the one you've been
validating.** You've been building a *free retail* halal screener for the HK
Muslim community (with a 30-day share-based exit gate). Chong is effectively
saying: **make it a paid B2B data/index business** — sell to institutions (funds,
banks, MPF), subscription model, Bloomberg-style, register a company, build a
team, expand to the Middle East.

That's not wrong — it may be a *better* business — but it's a pivot in customer,
model, and identity. Worth deciding consciously, not drifting. (More in §8.)

## ADDENDUM (clarified round 2) — direction, institutions, product map

### A. Direction — CONFIRMED: dual paid model
- **Both** an **institutional** version and a **retail** version, **both charging
  subscription**, with a **big upcharge for institutions.** Not free-retail any

  - **Retail → South-East Asia + India** (NOT HK — HK retail TAM too small).

### B. Institutional targets — detailed analysis
*(Grounded in current sources — see bottom. Realistic role for Mizan in all
three: the screening / index / data engine behind someone else's product, not
the regulated fund operator. That's a feature, not a limitation — the fund
manager bears Shariah responsibility, you provide the rigor.)*

**1. MPF (Mandatory Provident Fund) — the standout**
- **The gap is real and named:** there is currently **no Shariah-compliant MPF
  option**, leaving *hundreds of thousands* of HK Muslims with compulsory pension
  contributions and no halal fund to put them in. The HK govt itself frames this
  as inconsistent with its Islamic-finance ambitions.
- **Your realistic play:** license Mizan's screening/index to an **MPF trustee or
  fund house** so they can launch a Shariah-compliant constituent fund — Mizan as
  the index/screening provider behind it. You are NOT the fund manager (that's
  heavily regulated by MPFA/SFC).
- **Tailwinds:** govt pushing Islamic finance; "Islamic Finance Specialist" now on
  the HK talent list; eMPF platform modernising the system.
- **Honest caveats:** (a) others are already publicly floating "Islamic finance ×
  MPF" — so move, but (b) this is a **slow, regulated, relationship-heavy sale**
  (6–18 months), not quick revenue. Treat as a flagship lighthouse deal, not cash
  flow.

**2. Standard Chartered (Saadiq) — warm-ish institutional**
- SC runs a global Islamic-banking brand (**Saadiq**) and is reportedly getting
  **rising sukuk inquiries in HK** — an active player with Middle-East depth.
- **Your play:** license screening/index to their wealth / Islamic-finance desk,
  or supply the compliance engine for a screened equity product.
- **Note the earlier tension:** in the meeting he first called SC "premature,"
  then emphasised approaching them. Read: approach, but expect a longer courtship
  than MPF-fund-house conversations.

**3. HKMA (Hong Kong Monetary Authority) — door-opener, not a customer**
- HK's de-facto central bank; has **issued government sukuk** and actively
  promotes HK as an Islamic-finance hub; the Financial Secretary is publicly
  pushing this.
- **Your play:** not a paying client — a **credibility + introductions** channel.
  Govt initiatives, talent schemes, possibly grants/competitions. Being aligned
  with their agenda makes every other door easier.

### C. The index-fund / ROI thesis (this is now central)
Chong's frame: **Muslim fund investors mostly care about ROI.** So the product
isn't just "is it halal" — it's "**here's halal exposure, and here's the
return.**"
- **Halal index / index fund(s)** with **multiple risk/growth tiers** so investors
  pick their risk appetite.
- **ROI comparison vs conventional indexes** (e.g. "halal index vs S&P 500" over
  time) — show you don't sacrifice much (or where you do).
- **"Higher threshold to play safe"** now makes sense: for an *index/fund product*
  you screen **stricter** so the fund is unambiguously halal (no borderline names
  that could embarrass it) — different from the screener, which shows the full
  spectrum.
- **Portfolio builder v2** — you already have this, but the UI/UX is "sloppy and
  static." Upgrade: real past-performance tracking + benchmarking against indexes.
- **Real stock charts + technical analysis** (MA, RSI…) — Chong's own research
  area, so he values it; helps investors who care about timing/returns.

### D. AI feature opportunities (you asked where this fits)
- **AI halal Q&A** — you have `/ask`; extend it to portfolio-level questions.
- **AI compliance reports** — auto-generate a sourced "is this stock/fund/portfolio
  halal + why" report. *This is the institutional deliverable* (sell as a
  consulting output).
- **AI portfolio assistant** — "your portfolio is 80% compliant; here are halal
  swaps that keep your risk/return profile" (you already have a HalalAlternatives
  component to build on).
- **AI + technical analysis** — summarise chart signals in plain English.
- **⚠ Liability line:** anything that says "buy/sell" or recommends is investment
  advice (regulated). Keep AI framed as a *research assistant*, not advice — same
  disclaimer you already use.

### E. Naming
- Chong: the name should **instantly say what it does, like "Facebook."** "Mizan"
  reads unfamiliar / uncorrelated to Islamic finance for anyone who doesn't know
  the Arabic. (halal.com was *your* example note, not a fixed choice.)
- **Decision criteria:** instantly legible to a non-Arabic speaker, .com
  available, not trademark-conflicting, works for both retail + institutional.
  Worth a proper naming pass before you register the company — the registered name
  is what institutions will pay.
