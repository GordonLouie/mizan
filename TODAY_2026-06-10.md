## Should you roll out signup? — Not yet, and not as a wall.

Honest answer: a signup gate right now would hurt you. Your entire trust pitch is
"no signup, no data collected, your watchlist stays on your device" — the direct
answer to the AI-data-anxiety Anas flagged. Putting an account wall in front of a
product that hasn't proven retention yet would suppress the one healthy number you
have (42% landing → screen) and contradict your positioning. Wrong move at this
stage.

**When you do add accounts, the rule is: value first, account optional, core never
gated.** Concretely:

- Keep the **entire screener free and account-free**, forever. Anyone can search any
  ticker, see all 6 verdicts, read the citations — zero friction. This is your
  preview, and it stays fully open.
- Add an **optional, lightweight account** only for things that genuinely need it:
  syncing your watchlist/portfolio across devices, and **opt-in earnings alerts**
  ("email me before a holding reports"). Surface it *after* someone has built a
  watchlist — "Want this saved across devices? Add an email, no password" (magic
  link). Never before they've gotten value.
- The one capture worth adding *now* (and it's not a signup): a single **email field
  on the ticker-not-found state** and an **opt-in alerts toggle** — one input, no
  account, no data beyond an email they volunteer. Builds a list, closes the loop,
  keeps the promise.

So: don't build auth this sprint. If you want lead capture, add the single optional
email field. Full accounts come later, value-gated, and the screener preview is
never behind them.

## Cyberport — why "No application available now"

Investigated. It's not a bug or an account problem — the CIP application form is only
**open during fixed intake windows** (applications are batched ~3×/year), and you're
currently *between* windows, so the portal shows nothing to apply to.

- The most recent deadline (**1 Apr 2026**) has passed.
- **Next window: the August intake — application deadline ~3 Aug 2026** (vetting Sep,
  results Oct). After that, **1 Dec 2026**.
- The form for the Aug intake simply isn't live yet; check back in July or email
  **cip_enquiry@cyberport.hk** to confirm when it opens.

**Before you pursue it, one eligibility reality:** CIP requires a **Hong Kong–
incorporated digital-tech company** (or incorporating within 30 days of the
deadline), founders holding ≥51%, and a plan to be market-ready in 12–18 months.
Mizan isn't incorporated yet. So CIP would mean incorporating Mizan as a HK Ltd
first. Worth it *if* you decide (post-Day-18) to keep going — the grant is up to
HKD 500k. The earlier-stage **CCMF** (Creative Micro Fund, ~HKD 100k) is the usual
stepping stone and a lighter lift. Don't let this distract from the validation
sprint; park it as "if we continue, target the Aug CIP / next CCMF."
