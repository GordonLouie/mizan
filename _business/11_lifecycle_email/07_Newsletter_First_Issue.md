**Subject:** Why I'm writing this newsletter

Hi {first_name},

This is the first edition of the Mizan newsletter. Before I tell you what I'll be writing about, let me tell you why I'm writing at all.

Halal investing in English suffers from a particular kind of content drought. You can find:

- App-marketing posts ("5 reasons to use [our app] this week").
- General-finance posts that mention halal as an afterthought.
- Academic Islamic-finance literature that requires a PhD to read.

What you can't find: thoughtful, current, plain-language analysis of the actual decisions Muslim investors are making — and the scholarly variation that turns those decisions into puzzles.

So I'm going to write that. Every month, one essay. Some examples I'm planning:

- **Why Mufti Taqi Usmani's illiquid-asset rule excludes Amazon (and why it matters).**
- **The cost of using a halal app that doesn't match your scholar — quantified.**
- **What changed in Malaysia's screening rules in 2025 and how it affects everyone, not just Malaysians.**
- **Crypto, DeFi, and the scholar debate — an honest map.**
- **Hong Kong as the next Islamic-finance hub: why it might happen and why it might not.**

If that sounds useful, you're in the right place. If not, [the unsubscribe link is at the bottom of every email](mailto:hello@mizan.app?subject=unsubscribe) and I won't take it personally.

Welcome.
