Mufti Taqi Usmani — the most widely-cited contemporary Hanafi scholar — has been publishing this rule since the 1980s:

> A company's tangible (illiquid) assets should be at least 20% of total assets.

Why it matters: under his published methodology, this is a hard requirement. A company that's mostly cash, receivables, and intangibles fails — even if the standard interest and debt ratios are within limits.

If you follow Mufti Taqi Usmani — and millions of Muslim investors do — your portfolio may include holdings that aren't actually permissible under his rulings.

This isn't an indictment of those apps. They made a defensible product choice. But it's the gap Mizan was built to close.
