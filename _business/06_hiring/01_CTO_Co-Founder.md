## Why Mizan exists

Two billion Muslims globally. Every halal-investing app — Wahed, Zoya, Musaffa, Islamicly — screens against one scholarly standard (AAOIFI). But Muslims follow many scholars whose published rulings differ. The most-cited contemporary Hanafi scholar, Mufti Taqi Usmani, requires an illiquid-asset floor that no major app implements. Strict scholars hold zero-tolerance views. Ja'fari Shia have a different framework. We're building the first product that respects that variety. Working prototype lives at mizan.app — try `/screen?ticker=AMZN` for the pitch in 60 seconds.

## What you'd build

- **Engine v1 already exists.** TypeScript multi-scholar screening engine, six profiles, purification calculator, portfolio scoring. Tests pass. You don't start from scratch.
- **Production data pipeline.** Wire Financial Modeling Prep, graduate to Refinitiv/SIX at scale. Daily ingest, normalize, screen, alert on filings.
- **AI scholar Q&A.** Anthropic Claude with bounded scope, scholar-board-reviewed system prompts, citation-first answers, audit logging.
- **Mobile.** React Native shell sharing engine code with web. Ship iOS + Android by month 9.
- **Brokerage sync.** Plaid US, TrueLayer UK/EU. Read-only portfolio analysis, weighted compliance reporting.
