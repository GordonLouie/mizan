## Universal hiring principles

1. **No agencies, no recruiters.** First 15 hires through founder network + content-driven inbound. If you can't recruit them, you're not yet known enough — fix that first.
2. **Remote-first.** Only founder + CTO physically in HK. Everyone else where talent is best per dollar.
3. **References before offers.** Two backchannel checks per hire, not just listed references.
4. **Founder writes the spec.** Hand off only after the first 15.
5. **Always include a paid trial.** A two-week paid trial avoids most hiring mistakes.

## What goes in every JD

- The mission and the differentiator (why they should care about Mizan specifically).
- The honest version of what they'll spend their time on.
- The honest version of what's missing or hard.
- Comp band, equity range, paid trial process.
- One-paragraph description of the team they'll join.
- Application instructions: short Loom + one written question. No coverletters.
