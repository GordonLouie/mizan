# Senior Product Designer — Mizan  ·  Fully remote

**The role.** Own the entire surface of Mizan as a product — web, mobile, marketing site, in-app onboarding, the visual language of compliance. Halal investing apps default to dry, regulator-style UI. The opportunity here is to make Islamic finance feel as well-considered as Linear or Stripe.

## What you'd ship in your first 90 days

- **Design system v1.** Tokens, components, mobile patterns. Document it in Figma. Make it the source of truth for engineering.
- **Mobile app interaction model.** Mizan ships web first; mobile is the wedge to mass market. You design the React Native shell from first principles.
- **Compliance verdict UX.** The core interaction. How do you make a stock's pass/fail with five ratio breakdowns feel honest, fast, and not anxiety-producing? Iterate with users.
- **Onboarding for the "pick your scholar" flow.** This is the moment of first delight or first confusion. Get it right.

## Who I'm looking for

- 5+ years product design, with at least one consumer fintech product shipped and lived-with in production.
- Strong visual sense — your portfolio shows craft, not just process diagrams.
- Comfortable working with data, financial concepts, and progressive disclosure.
- Pragmatic about Tailwind and shadcn/ui — uses the tools that ship fastest, customizes carefully.
- Has worked alongside engineers in a shipping cadence; doesn't need a separate design-PR cycle.
- Sensitive to the religious context. You don't need to be Muslim, but you need to respect that this product touches something users care about beyond utility.
