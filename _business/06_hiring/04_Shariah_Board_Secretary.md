# Shariah Board Secretary — Mizan  ·  Fully remote

**The role.** Be the operational backbone of Mizan's Shariah Supervisory Board. You'll work directly with the three named scholars (Chair, US Member, Ja'fari Member), the founder, and the product team — bridging scholarship and shipping cadence. This is a full-time role with real influence on the methodology that millions of Muslim investors will eventually rely on.

## What you'd own

- **Pre-release methodology reviews.** Every product release that touches a screening preset, AI scholar Q&A scope, or methodology display goes through you first. You triage what needs scholar review and what's clearly clear.
- **Quarterly board meetings.** Agenda, minutes, follow-up actions, public summary publication.
- **AAOIFI + scholarly updates monitoring.** AAOIFI publishes annually. Mufti Taqi publishes occasionally. SAC Malaysia publishes rulings. You read it all, summarize for the board, and propose product changes.
- **Annual public Shariah report.** You ghostwrite the first draft; scholars co-sign the final.
- **User-raised concerns.** When a paying user disputes a verdict on religious grounds, you triage and decide whether to escalate.

## Who I'm looking for

- AAOIFI-certified Shariah auditor OR equivalent (e.g., CISI Islamic Finance Qualification, IFAA, INCEIF graduate, or comparable institutional credential).
- 3+ years working inside an Islamic finance institution (bank, fund, fintech, advisor).
- Multilingual: Arabic + English minimum. Urdu, Farsi, or Bahasa a strong plus.
- Strong writer — much of this role is producing clear written summaries for non-scholar audiences.
- Comfortable working at startup pace. We ship weekly; you'll triage what's "must review now" vs "next quarter."
- Religiously practising. This role is the conscience of the product. It needs to be someone whose conscience the user can implicitly trust.

## Comp

- **$50K base** (remote, location-adjusted; flexible if you're located in the GCC where market rates are higher).
- **0.5% equity** (3-year vest, 1-year cliff).
- Full remote. Quarterly board meeting in person (rotating: London, Houston, HK).
