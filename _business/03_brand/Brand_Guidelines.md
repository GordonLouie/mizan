# Mizan brand guidelines  ·  v1.0

## The name

**Mizan** (الميزان) — Arabic for "balance" or "scale". A Quranic concept of measured judgment and justice. The name evokes the product thesis directly: every investor calibrates compliance against their own scholar's standard, and Mizan is the instrument they use to do it.

Pronounced: **mee-ZAHN** (rhymes with "we ran"). Stress on the second syllable.

## Voice

- **Confident, not loud.** We speak with the authority that comes from being the only product in the space that takes scholarly diversity seriously. We don't shout.
- **Plain, not jargon.** "Interest income" not "riba-based revenue streams". "Your scholar's ruling" not "the chosen Shariah methodology framework". Make Islamic finance feel like a normal, dignified, accessible part of life.
- **Reverent toward scholarship, blunt about product.** When discussing scholars we use their full honorifics (Mufti, Sheikh, Ayatollah). When discussing software we say what it does.
- **Never preachy.** We never tell users what's halal or haram. We tell them what their scholar says, and we show our work.

## Tagline

**Halal investing, calibrated to your conscience.**

Secondary lines that work in different surfaces:
- "Your scholar. Your portfolio. The same answer." (product positioning)
- "Six scholars. One app." (acquisition headline)
- "We show the math." (trust positioning)

### Typography

| Use | Font | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Headers | **Georgia** (serif) | Communicates scholarship, gravitas. |
| Body | **Calibri** or **Inter** | Reads as modern, accessible, friendly. |
| Numbers (financial) | Calibri | Tabular figures preferred. |

Use Georgia for the wordmark and major page headings only — body text should always be sans-serif.

### Logo construction

The mark is a stylized balance (mizan) inside a navy rounded square. The two pans are intentionally unequal — the left pan hangs lower (more weight, the stricter scholar; the right pan tips up (the more permissive scholar). The asymmetry is the brand: there is no single correct verdict.

**Files:**
- `logo_mark.svg` — square mark only, 256×256
- `logo_wordmark.svg` — mark + wordmark + tagline, 720×200
- `favicon.svg` — 32×32 simplified mark
- `og_image.svg` — 1200×630 social-link-preview image

**Clear space:** Keep at least the height of the mark's gold beam as clear space around the logo on all sides.

**Don'ts:**
- Don't recolor the mark. Navy + gold + white only.
- Don't add religious imagery (crescents, stars, calligraphic Allahu Akbar). The brand is intentionally non-iconographic so it reads as a financial-services product, not a religious icon.
- Don't stretch or skew the logo.
- Don't put the logo on a busy background. Always navy-on-light or white-on-navy.

## Things we do and don't say

| ✓ Use | ✗ Don't use |
|-------|-------------|
| "Halal investing" | "Islamic investing" (less precise) |
| "Compliance" or "verdict" | "Halal certified" (we don't certify) |
| "Scholar" or "ruling" | "Fatwa" in marketing (too inside-baseball) |
| "Purify" | "Cleanse" (loaded word) |
| "Riba" only when explaining a specific concept | Default to "interest" for accessibility |
| "Mufti Taqi Usmani" with full honorific | First-name informality |

## Tagline rules

- Always italics for the tagline.
- Period at the end, not exclamation.
- Never translate the tagline into Arabic in English contexts.
- Arabic copy uses the same brand voice — confident, accessible.

## In one sentence

Mizan is the brand of a product that respects users enough to show its scholarly work — visually calm, textually careful, and unapologetic about being the only player that takes the breadth of Islamic jurisprudence seriously.
