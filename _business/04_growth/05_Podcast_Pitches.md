# Podcast outreach — 8 target shows + drafted pitches

**Why podcasts.** Long-form audio is where halal-finance audiences live. One 60-minute podcast with the right host beats 30 Twitter threads.

**Cadence.** Pitch 2 per week for the first 4 weeks. Aim to be on 4 of these 8 within 90 days.

---

## 1. Islamic Finance Guru (IFG) podcast — Mohsin Patel + Ibrahim Khan

**Why this one first.** Largest English-language halal finance audience. Founders are friendly, build-in-public, will give you 60 honest minutes.

**Pitch (email):**

Subject: Mizan founder — pitch for the IFG podcast

Hi Mohsin / Ibrahim,

IFG is what got me thinking seriously about halal investing as a product space — your screener walkthrough video from 2021 led me down a rabbit hole that ended with me starting Mizan.

Mizan (mizan.app) is the first stock screener that adapts to the user's scholar — AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani, DJIM, Ja'fari/Sistani-aligned, strict zero-tolerance, or fully custom. The demo that closes meetings is AMZN passing AAOIFI but failing Mufti Taqi's illiquid-asset floor — a rule no major app currently implements.

I'd love to come on IFG to walk through the product, the scholar-board strategy, and what I think the next 5 years of halal fintech looks like. Happy to debate or be challenged.

Best,
jk

## 2. The Muslim Money Show — Iqram Magdon-Ismail

**Why.** US-based, more consumer-focused. Audience is the wedge persona.

Pitch reuses #1 with US-flavoured framing: emphasize US diaspora audience, 401(k) gap.

---

## 3. Muslim Founder Podcast

**Why.** Founder-to-founder audience. Good for warm investor intros.

Pitch: emphasize the building-in-public story, the HK angle, the Shariah-board strategy.

---

## 4. The Mad Mamluks

**Why.** Massive English-language Muslim audience, secular-tilted, smart hosts.

Pitch: lead with "Muslims have been let down by halal investing apps that pretend Islamic jurisprudence has one answer when it has six." Frame it as cultural critique not product pitch.

---

## 5. Islamic Finance Podcast — IFN

**Why.** Industry insider audience. Investors and bankers listen.

Pitch: emphasize the institutional angle. AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi, supervisory governance. Less consumer story, more "the next layer in Islamic fintech."

---

## 6. Halal Money Matters — Saturna / Amana

**Why.** Established institutional voice. Reaching their audience legitimizes Mizan to older / more conservative segments.

Pitch: collaborative tone. We're not competing with mutual funds, we're the screening layer above them.

---

## 7. The Halal Vibes — IFG-adjacent

**Why.** Lifestyle audience, young, diaspora.

Pitch: lifestyle framing — "everyone in your DMs uses a different halal app and gets different answers. Here's why."

---

## 8. Practical Islamic Finance — Rakaan Kayali

**Why.** Engaged YouTube + podcast audience. He invests himself, will ask sharp questions.

Pitch: direct, technical. "I want to show your audience why every halal app screens the same way and what we're doing differently."

## Followup discipline

- Day 0: send pitch.
- Day 7: one-line bump if no reply.
- Day 14: move on. Try again in 6 months when you have more traction.
- Track in spreadsheet alongside VCs.
