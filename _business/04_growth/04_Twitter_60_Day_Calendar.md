# X / Twitter — 60-day content calendar

**Strategy.** Mizan's X account is jk's secondary growth channel after YouTube. Goal: 5K followers in 60 days through three plays — original threads, reply-guy strategy on every viral halal-finance tweet, and reaction takes on news.

**Cadence.** One scheduled tweet per day minimum. One thread per week minimum. Reply to 5–10 tweets daily in the halal-finance, fintech, and personal-finance worlds.

**Voice.** Same as the brand: confident, specific, never preachy. Numbers, screenshots, and named scholars. Avoid hot takes on doctrine.

## What NOT to tweet

- Doctrinal hot takes on which scholar is "right".
- Anything sectarian (Sunni vs Shia, Hanafi vs Hanbali).
- Hot takes on geopolitics, no matter how tempting.
- "I think AAOIFI is wrong about X" — let scholars debate scholars; you're product.
- Anything Israel/Palestine. Stay focused. Founders who lose narrative discipline here lose customers.
