# Pre-seed VC outreach — 12 personalized cold emails

**How to use this file.** These are drafted for jk's voice. Send from `jk@mizan.app` once the domain + email are live. Personalize the first line per recipient (find a recent post or company news to reference) — that's the only thing that prevents these from looking like a mailmerge. Each email is under 200 words. Subject lines are designed to get opened, not to be clever.

**Send order.** Start with the warmest-lead funds (Hambro Perks, Zayn, Wamda). Save Y Combinator for last — apply via their form, not cold email.

**Tracking.** Use a spreadsheet with columns: Fund · Partner · Sent date · Opened · Replied · Meeting booked · Outcome. Don't trust your memory.
