# Masjid partnership program — pitch deck outline & talk track

**The thesis.** Masjids are the highest-trust distribution channel for any Muslim-consumer product. One imam's recommendation outperforms $10K of paid acquisition. But you can't be transactional with masjids — you have to give first.

**Offer.** Free Pro tier for the imam and staff. $4.99/seat for the congregation (vs $9.99/mo individual). The masjid hosts one financial-literacy event you teach; you sponsor refreshments.

**Pilot target.** 20 masjids in months 6–9, covering: NYC, NJ, Chicago, Houston, Toronto, London. Choose ones with active imams who use technology and have under-50 congregations of professionals.

---

## Talk track for the imam meeting

**Opening (2 min)**
- Brief intro: "I'm a Muslim founder building a stock screening app from Hong Kong. Before I tell you about the app, I want to ask: what financial questions do you get from your congregation that you wish you could answer better?"
- Listen genuinely. Don't pitch yet.

**The product, framed for them (5 min)**
- "Brothers and sisters in your community are buying stocks and they're not sure what's halal. Half of them follow Mufti Taqi Usmani. The apps they're using don't implement his rulings. They're investing on what amounts to incomplete information."
- "Mizan adapts to your scholar. Mufti Taqi's full rulings, AAOIFI, DJIM, Ja'fari — all visible side-by-side. Users pick whose rulings they follow and the verdicts adjust."

**The masjid offer (3 min)**
- "I'd love to make Mizan Pro free for you and your senior staff — permanently, no strings attached."
- "If you'd like, we'd discount Mizan Pro to $4.99/month for your congregation members — half the individual price. We're not asking for anything from you; if your members want it, great. If not, no harm."
- "I would also love to come (or send pre-recorded content) to teach a free financial literacy class for your community: 'How to invest the halal way.' I bring snacks. I don't sell anything during the class."

**The ask (1 min)**
- "Would you be open to a pilot — three months — where we offer this to your community and see if it's useful?"

---

## What you need them to do

1. Send a one-paragraph email to their distribution list with a link to mizan.app/masjid.
2. Display a QR code on the back of a flyer at Friday prayer (or pin a tweet, depending on the masjid).
3. Maybe mention it once in a khutbah if comfortable. Don't ask for this directly.

---

## What you do NOT do

- Do not pay the imam directly. Pay for refreshments at the literacy class, period.
- Do not turn the literacy class into a product pitch. It's free education with the brand as a watermark.
- Do not promise affiliate revenue.
- Do not push imams who say no. One "yes" out of ten is success.
