## Why Option B is the better send

Three reasons:

1. **It answers the literal question** (yes, halal-Yahoo-Finance is the goal)
   AND positions Mizan's actual differentiator (multi-scholar, primary
   sources) so he doesn't lump it in with Zoya / Musaffa / Wahed in his head.
2. **It asks him one specific feedback question** — "does multi-scholar feel
   useful to a real investor or is it noise?" This is a real product question
   you actually need answered, and giving him something specific to react to
   means he'll come back Monday with substance rather than vibes.
3. **It plants seeds for collaboration** without asking for anything yet. If
   he's connected in the halal-investing community, the natural next step
   after Monday is to ask if he knows anyone who'd want early access.

Avoid: oversharing the roadmap, listing every feature, name-dropping
"Cyberport" or fundraising. He's offering a 5-minute look; treat it that way.
