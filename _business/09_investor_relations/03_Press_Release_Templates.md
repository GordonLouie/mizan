# Press release templates

Use these as starting points. Customize headline, dateline, and lead. Boilerplate at the bottom of each is fixed — copy-paste exactly. Distribute via direct emails to journalists from the targeted-press list, not via wire services (unless company-wide milestone, in which case Business Wire or PR Newswire is fine).

---

## Template 1: Company formation

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Mizan launches in Hong Kong to bring scholarly diversity to halal investing**

*Multi-scholar stock screening platform addresses a gap in $5T Islamic finance market*

**HONG KONG — [DATE]** — Mizan, a new Hong Kong-based fintech, launched today to address a long-standing gap in halal investing: existing apps screen every user against the same Shariah methodology despite the global Muslim audience following many different scholars with materially different rulings.

The company's first product, available in early access at mizan.app, ships with six pre-built scholar profiles — including AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani's stricter Hanafi rulings, Dow Jones Islamic Market, a Ja'fari (Shia) methodology aligned with Ayatollah Sistani's published rulings, and a customizable Custom profile. Users select their scholar and the product calibrates every compliance verdict accordingly, showing the underlying financial ratios transparently rather than as a black-box score.

"Most halal-investing apps treat Shariah compliance as a binary," said jk, founder and CEO. "It's not. There's an entire spectrum of scholarly thresholds, and each user has a right to the verdict their own scholar would give. Mufti Taqi Usmani's published rulings, for example, require an illiquid-asset floor that no existing app implements — meaning users who follow him are receiving misleading verdicts elsewhere."

Mizan is incorporated as Mizan Operations (HK) Limited, with Mizan Holdings Ltd as the Cayman Islands holding company. The company is currently in the Cyberport Incubation Programme and is finalizing its Shariah Supervisory Board.

For interviews or more information, contact press@mizan.app.

## Distribution checklist

- [ ] Embargo time agreed with at least one target outlet (gives them first-look story).
- [ ] Founder photo + product screenshots attached.
- [ ] One-page fact sheet attached.
- [ ] Quote(s) pre-approved by quoted parties (especially scholars).
- [ ] Translation prepared if expanding into MENA / Indonesia / Pakistan (Arabic, Bahasa, Urdu).
- [ ] Sent to direct journalist list (not wire) for first 6 hours; wire after.
- [ ] Social posts scheduled to publish 30 minutes after embargo lifts.
- [ ] Investor update written and sent same day (with link to coverage as it appears).
