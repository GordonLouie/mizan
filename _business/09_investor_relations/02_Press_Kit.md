## Boilerplate

**Short (one sentence)**

Mizan is the first stock screener that adapts to the user's scholar, with six built-in Shariah methodologies — including rulings from Mufti Taqi Usmani — plus a customizable profile and AI scholar Q&A.

**Medium (one paragraph)**

Mizan is a Hong Kong-based fintech building the first stock screener that adapts to each user's preferred scholar instead of defaulting to a single Shariah standard. The product ships with six scholar profiles — AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani's stricter Hanafi rulings, Dow Jones Islamic Market, Ja'fari (Sistani-aligned), strict zero-tolerance, and a fully customizable profile — alongside an AI scholar Q&A grounded in board-reviewed scholarly reasoning. The product addresses a gap left by existing halal-investing apps, which screen every user against the same default standard despite Muslims globally following different scholars whose published rulings differ materially.

**Long (one page)**

Two billion Muslims worldwide, plus 50 million in Western diaspora, currently use halal-investing apps that screen stocks against a single Shariah methodology. Mufti Taqi Usmani, the most-cited contemporary Hanafi scholar, requires an illiquid-asset floor of at least 20% — a rule that surfaces a meaningfully different verdict on companies like Amazon, Meta, and Netflix than the default AAOIFI screen produces. Strict scholars take a zero-tolerance view. Ja'fari Shia operate under a different framework altogether. Today, none of these distinctions are represented in mainstream halal-investing software.

Mizan was founded in 2026 in Hong Kong to close this gap. The product ships with six pre-built scholar profiles, an AI scholar Q&A system grounded in scholar-vetted reasoning, automated dividend and capital-gain purification calculations, brokerage portfolio sync, and a transparent ratio breakdown for every compliance verdict. The Shariah Supervisory Board includes Mufti Faraz Adam (UK Hanafi, Chair), Sheikh Joe Bradford (US Hanbali/Maliki), and an Al-Mahdi Institute Ja'fari scholar — bringing scholarly governance to consumer halal-fintech for the first time at this depth.

The company is headquartered in Hong Kong (operating entity Mizan Operations (HK) Limited; holding company Mizan Holdings Ltd, Cayman Islands), with global ambitions across Western Muslim diaspora markets, South Asia, Southeast Asia, and the Gulf Cooperation Council.

## What we are, and what we're not

**Mizan is:**
- A halal-investing research tool, helping users determine whether a stock is permissible under their chosen scholar's methodology.
- A platform that surfaces multiple scholarly opinions on the same stock, with transparent reasoning.
- A consumer product, priced for individual investors.
- Operationally independent of any particular bank, brokerage, or asset manager.

**Mizan is not:**
- An investment advisor. We do not issue buy/sell recommendations.
- A bank or broker-dealer. We do not hold user funds or execute trades.
- A robo-advisor. We do not manage portfolios.
- A religious authority. Compliance verdicts in Mizan are scholarly opinions, not certifications.
