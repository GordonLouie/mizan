## SECTION 1 — Idea

Mizan is a stock screening and halal investing platform that adapts to the user's scholar (Islamic religious authority). Every existing halal-investing app screens stocks against a single AAOIFI standard. Mizan ships with six scholar profiles — AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani, Dow Jones Islamic Market, Ja'fari (Sistani-aligned), strict zero-tolerance, and a user-customizable profile — and re-renders every compliance verdict against the user's chosen scholar.

The differentiator: Mufti Taqi Usmani's illiquid-asset floor (≥20% of total assets) is not implemented by any major existing app. Surfacing it eliminates ~40% of the S&P 500 (including Amazon, Meta, Netflix) for users who follow him.

---

## SECTION 2 — Problem & opportunity

**Problem:** 2 billion Muslims globally; ~50 million in Western diaspora with disposable income. Every halal-investing product they use returns a single verdict against one scholar's standard. If they follow a different scholar, the verdict is misleading. The result: investors are making religiously-significant decisions based on incomplete information.

**Market size:**
- $5.2T Islamic finance assets globally, 2025.
- Islamic fintech segment $341B by 2029.
- Mizan's beachhead (US/UK/Canada Hanafi diaspora): ~5M people, ~500K willingness-to-pay overlap.

---

## SECTION 3 — Solution & innovation

A consumer-facing web and mobile screener that:
1. Computes Shariah compliance verdicts under multiple scholarly methodologies simultaneously.
2. Shows the underlying ratio math transparently (no black-box scores).
3. Lets users build custom screening profiles by setting their own thresholds.
4. Calculates purification (charitable donation) requirements for impermissible dividend/gain income.
5. Provides AI-powered Q&A grounded in scholar-reviewed reasoning.

**Innovation:** Multi-scholar as architecture, not toggle. Named-scholar supervisory board governing the methodology.

## SECTION 6 — Why Hong Kong

Hong Kong is the only major financial center with an active mandate (via HKMA) to promote Islamic finance. Direct flight access to all Mizan's expansion markets — MENA (Dubai, Riyadh), SE Asia (Jakarta, KL), South Asia (Karachi). 300K Muslim residents in HK and a wealthy Pakistani-origin business community for early angel investment.

Tax: HK's territorial system means SaaS revenue earned outside HK can qualify for the offshore profits exemption — material to a global product.
