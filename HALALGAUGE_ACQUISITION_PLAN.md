# HalalGauge — Customer Acquisition Plan (0 → first 1,000 users)

*Honest framing up front: nothing guarantees success. What follows is the highest-probability, lowest-cost plan for HalalGauge specifically — built around two unfair advantages you actually have, and designed so you find out fast what works instead of betting everything on a guess.*

---

## Your two unfair advantages (build everything on these)

1. **You answer what people already search and ask.** "Is [stock] halal", "is bitcoin halal", "halal ETFs", "how do I purify haram income" — this is enormous, constant, high-intent demand. You don't need to create desire; you need to *show up* where it already exists. Most startups have to manufacture demand. You don't.
2. **You're an insider.** You're a Muslim med student. You have MSAs, mosques, WhatsApp groups, and community trust that no outside founder can buy. Warm distribution is your cheat code.

The whole plan is: point these two advantages at a funnel, measure ruthlessly, and double down on whatever channel returns users.

---

## The one rule: don't spread thin

At zero users you do **not** run ten channels. You nail **one primary channel to first traction**, prove it, then layer the next. For HalalGauge the primary channel is **high-intent search + community seeding** — because that's where your two advantages compound. Everything else is secondary until the primary works.

**North-star metric:** *activated users* — someone who set a scholar AND screened at least one stock (or built a portfolio). Not signups, not visits. Activation, because that's the moment they got value and might come back. Track it from day one.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundations (this week, before any outreach)

You're driving people somewhere, so make sure it converts and compounds.

- **Programmatic SEO — the single biggest lever.** Turn every stock verdict into an indexable page titled *"Is [Company] halal? — HalalGauge"* at a clean URL (e.g. `/halal/AAPL`), server-rendered with the verdict, ratios, and sources in the initial HTML. You already compute 650+ verdicts — that's potentially 650 landing pages each targeting a real search query, for near-zero marginal effort. This is a compounding asset that pays off for years. *(If `/halal/[ticker]` already exists, the job is: confirm it's server-rendered, titled as a question, and internally linked.)*
- **Make sharing effortless.** The "share verdict" card, the asset map, and a "your portfolio is X% halal" result should each produce a clean image/link people *want* to post. Product-led sharing is free acquisition.
- **Instrument everything.** Analytics on activation + per-channel source (UTM links). You can't double down on what you can't see.
- **One feedback line.** A visible "give feedback" path so your first 100 users become your product team and your testimonials.

## Phase 1 — Manual, unscalable seeding (Weeks 1–4, target: 0 → 100 activated)

Do things that don't scale. Go where Muslims already discuss money and be genuinely useful — answer the question first, mention the tool only when it's the actual answer.

- **Reddit:** r/IslamicFinance, r/MuslimLounge, r/Muslim, r/Bogleheads-adjacent threads where "is X halal" comes up. Read each sub's rules; lead with a genuinely helpful answer, link the specific verdict page, never spam.
- **Discord / Telegram / WhatsApp:** halal-investing and Muslim-finance servers and diaspora groups. These convert *much* higher than cold social because there's existing trust.
- **X / Twitter:** reply to "is X halal" questions and Muslim FinTwit threads with the specific answer + your page. Follow and engage the halal-investing conversation daily.
- **Facebook groups:** "Halal Investing", "Muslim Investors", country-specific ones (huge in the US, UK, Malaysia, Indonesia).

Goal isn't just users — it's **learning which message and which community converts**, plus 5–10 quotable testimonials.

## Phase 2 — Creators + build-in-public (Weeks 4–12, target: 100 → 1,000)

- **Creator partnerships are your accelerant.** There's a whole genre of halal-investing YouTubers, TikTokers and Instagram creators. Offer the free tool for an honest review/demo, a custom link, and (later) an affiliate/referral cut. One mid-size creator doing "I screened my whole portfolio with this" can outdo months of grinding. Start with micro-creators (5–50k) — they reply and convert better than big names.
- **Build in public.** Post the journey on X/LinkedIn — the transparency mission, the "no black box" stance, the scholar conversations, what you're learning. Founders who narrate the build attract early believers and press. Your Frankel relationship (once appropriate) is a credibility story worth telling.
- **Content engine.** Turn the asset map, crypto framework, and income explorer into short posts/threads/videos. The "periodic table of halal assets" is inherently shareable — pitch it to Islamic-finance newsletters and LinkedIn.

## Phase 3 — Compounding loops (Month 3+)

- **SEO matures.** Your programmatic pages start ranking; this becomes your largest, cheapest, always-on channel. Keep publishing education targeting real queries.
- **Referral loop.** "Invite a friend to check their portfolio" — Muslims share halal tools within families and communities readily.
- **Campus + mosque (your warm channel).** MSA financial-literacy nights, a mosque talk on halal investing, a table at Islamic events. High-density, high-trust, essentially free — and uniquely available to you.
- **Ramadan timing.** Purification, zakat and charity are top-of-mind in Ramadan. Plan a content + feature push around it every year; it's your natural seasonal spike.

---

## What NOT to do (these waste money/time at zero users)

- **No paid ads yet.** You don't know your message or your converting audience. Paid ads before product-market signal just buys expensive proof you're not ready. Revisit once a channel converts organically.
- **Don't spray all channels at once.** Nail one, then add.
- **Don't overclaim.** You have no scholar sign-off yet — lead with *transparency* ("free, shows its work, cites every source"), never with authority you don't have. A single overstated religious claim destroys trust permanently in this community.

---

## Weekly cadence (keep it simple)

Each week: (1) publish/optimise a few SEO pages or one piece of content, (2) show up daily in 2–3 communities, (3) reach out to 3–5 creators, (4) look at the numbers and cut what's dead. Every Friday: which channel produced the most *activated* users? Do more of that next week.

## Milestones (realistic, not promised)

- **Month 1:** 100 activated users, 5+ testimonials, one community that clearly converts.
- **Month 3:** ~1,000 users, one working repeatable channel, first creator collab live.
- **Month 6–12:** SEO compounding to the majority of traffic; a referral loop; a Ramadan push.

---

## The biggest risk (say it plainly)

Acquisition into a **leaky bucket** is wasted. If people screen one stock and never return, no channel saves you. Your retention is the scholar-calibrated portfolio + drift watch + purification — the sticky core you've already built. **Acquisition and retention are one system:** bring people in *and* give them a reason to come back. And the deeper trust ceiling is still the credentialed scholar — it caps how far word-of-mouth and creator credibility can carry you. Growth buys time; the scholar buys legitimacy. Do both.
