# Outreach playbook — Tier 1: seed 30 testers

The product is live and operational. The next job is finding the first 30
real Muslim investors who'll use it, react, and (the good ones) tell their
friends. This document is the working playbook.

Edit as you learn what works. Track wins and losses. Update the response
log daily.

---

## The goal, precisely

30 hand-picked Muslim investors who:

1. Receive a personal DM from you (not a broadcast post)
2. Open the link and try Mizan on a stock they actually own
3. Reply with feedback (positive, negative, doesn't matter — engagement is the signal)
4. From those 30, target 3–5 evangelists who share Mizan with their network unprompted

**Two weeks. 5–7 DMs per weekday.** That's the budget. Hitting 30 should feel deliberate, not rushed.

**Why one-at-a-time and not a broadcast post**: posting "I built a halal stock screener!" on r/IslamicFinance with zero followers gets ignored. Personal DMs — especially with a specific stock to test — get 30%+ reply rates. After 30 personal users with stories, public broadcast actually works.

## DM templates

### Template A — The honest cold DM (default, use for most contacts)

```
Hey {name} — I'm Gordon, technologist in Hong Kong. Not Muslim.

A friend got me curious why existing halal investing apps don't show
how different scholars rule on the same stock. So I built one that does:
mizan.app

Free, no account. Try it on a stock you actually own — see all 6
scholarly verdicts side-by-side: mizan.app/screen?ticker=AAPL

If something's wrong I want to hear it. I read every reply personally.
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with honesty about who you are (non-Muslim, technologist)
- Names the gap their existing tools have
- One-click test, doesn't ask for anything
- Closes with a personal reply promise — distinguishes you from chatbot startups

**Tune per audience:**
- For someone in finance/tech, lead with "I built a halal stock screener that shows scholarly disagreement"
- For someone older / less tech-fluent, lead with "I made a free website that shows whether stocks pass halal screening under different scholars"

## When someone replies

**Read their feedback within 24h.** Reply personally — never with a template.

**If positive ("nice tool!"):** ask one specific follow-up question. "Did you try it on a stock you actually own? Any number look off?" — converts a vague positive into testable feedback.

**If negative ("X is wrong"):** thank them, fix within 48h if possible, and tell them publicly when fixed. Visible turnaround is the single biggest evangelism driver.

**If they ignore you:** don't follow up. The miss rate will be high (60-70%). Move on to the next.

**If they share Mizan with someone else (you find out from a screenshot, a DM, anything):** thank them privately. Ask if they'd be OK being publicly thanked when Mizan hits 100 users. Evangelists love public recognition; introverts hate it — let them choose.

## Daily rhythm

**Monday-Friday:**
1. Send 5-7 personalized DMs (45 mins)
2. Reply to any responses from prior days within 24h (15-30 mins)
3. Comment value on 3 Muslim-finance posts (X or Reddit) without dropping the link (15 mins)
4. Ship one product fix or improvement from feedback (rest of the time)

**Saturday:** rest. Read a few books on Islamic finance — Mufti Taqi Usmani's *Introduction to Islamic Finance* is the canonical one.

**Sunday:** review the tracker. Who hasn't replied? Who needs follow-up content? What patterns are emerging in feedback? Plan Monday's batch.

---

## Red lines — do NOT do these

1. **Don't post a cold "I built a halal investing app, please try it!" on a subreddit you haven't commented in.** Reddit punishes that fast and bans accounts permanently.
2. **Don't email purchased mailing lists.** Reputation kills.
3. **Don't claim a Shariah Supervisory Board until you have one in writing.** (The trust-up already removed this; keep the discipline.)
4. **Don't compare Mizan to other apps disparagingly in DMs.** "X app is wrong" loses you the people who use X app. "Different angle — shows all scholars" wins them.
5. **Don't promise paid features or specific dates.** Promises you can't keep destroy trust faster than missing features ever will.
6. **Don't reply to a negative DM defensively.** Thank them, fix the thing, follow up with the fix. Even one defensive reply gets screenshotted and circulated.

## What makes an evangelist

You'll know when it's happening. Signs:

- Someone DMs a friend a Mizan link without you asking, and the friend reaches you cold via that link
- Someone posts a screenshot of Mizan on their own X/Instagram/WhatsApp story
- Someone writes a long-form post or video about why halal investing apps are flawed, and Mizan gets cited
- Someone introduces you to a scholar or a community leader on their initiative

**When this happens:**
1. Privately thank them
2. Ship the next thing they suggested faster than they expected
3. When you have 100 users, write a public thank-you post naming the early evangelists (only with consent)

---

## After week 2

If the 30-person test succeeded — you have ~30 messages out, ~10-12 replies, ~5 active users, ~1-2 evangelists — graduate to Tier 2 (public social presence on X + Reddit).

If it didn't, the most likely reason is the product itself. Reread feedback. The fix is product, not more outreach. Don't double the outreach against a product people don't like — you'll just spread the dissatisfaction faster.
