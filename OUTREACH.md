# Outreach playbook — Tier 1: seed 30 testers

The product is live and operational at **https://mizaninvesting.com**. The next
job is finding the first 30 real Muslim investors who'll use it, react, and
(the good ones) tell their friends. This document is the working playbook.

Edit as you learn what works. Track wins and losses. Update the response
log daily.

---

## Exit criterion (pre-committed before emotional attachment)

**If by 30 days after first DM I have <3 unprompted shares — I exit.**
No second-guessing. No pivoting endlessly. The product premise lost.

Unprompted share = someone tells someone else about Mizan WITHOUT me asking
(forwards the link in their own DM, posts a screenshot, mentions it on a podcast,
introduces me to a scholar). The Council pre-committed me to this number so
emotion doesn't get to revise the bar later.

---

## The goal, precisely

30 hand-picked Muslim investors who:

1. Receive a personal DM from you (not a broadcast post)
2. Open the link and try Mizan on a stock they actually own
3. Reply with feedback (positive, negative, doesn't matter — engagement is the signal)
4. From those 30, target 3+ evangelists who share Mizan with their network unprompted

**Two weeks. 5–7 DMs per weekday.** That's the budget.

**Why one-at-a-time and not a broadcast post:** posting "I built a halal stock
screener!" on r/IslamicFinance with zero followers gets ignored. Personal DMs
— especially with a specific stock to test — get 30%+ reply rates. After 30
personal users with stories, public broadcast actually works.

## Better tester questions (replace generic "is it cool?")

The Council warned: "useful or confusing?" is leading. "Cool site!" replies
are polite, not useful. Use these instead. When someone replies to your DM,
send these three (verbatim) as the follow-up:

```
Three quick questions when you have a sec:

1. Walk me through what you actually did when you opened the site. First
   click, second click, where did you stop. (Not "what did you think" —
   what did you actually DO.)

2. Did you screen a stock you actually own? If yes — was the verdict what
   you expected? If no — why not?

3. If you described Mizan to one of your friends in ONE sentence, what
   would you say? Don't polish it — the messier the better.
```

Bonus question for engaged testers:
```
4. What's the ONE thing you'd change first? Doesn't have to be deep.
   First instinct.
```

**Why these work:** they force behavioral recall (#1), prove depth-of-use
(#2), and stress-test positioning (#3). The Council's data point so far is
that polite "cool site" feedback is the noise floor. You need to push past it.

## When someone replies

**Read their feedback within 24h.** Reply personally — never with a template.

**If positive ("nice tool!"):** ask the three follow-up questions above. Convert
vague positive into testable feedback.

**If negative ("X is wrong"):** thank them, fix within 48h if possible, and
tell them publicly when fixed. Visible turnaround is the single biggest
evangelism driver.

**If they ignore you:** don't follow up. Miss rate will be 60-70%. Move on.

**If they share Mizan unprompted:** thank privately. Ask if they'd be OK being
publicly thanked when Mizan hits 100 users. Track them in the tracker as
`EV` — these are the people whose count determines whether you continue or exit.

## Daily rhythm

**Monday-Friday:**
1. Send 5-7 personalized DMs (45 mins) — Hanafi-leaning contacts use Template A
2. Reply to any responses from prior days within 24h (15-30 mins)
3. Comment value on 3 Muslim-finance posts (X or Reddit) without dropping the link (15 mins)
4. Ship one product fix or improvement from feedback (rest of the time)

**Saturday:** rest. Read a few pages of Mufti Taqi Usmani's *Introduction to
Islamic Finance* — keeps you sharp on what your product is actually claiming.

**Sunday:** review the tracker. Who hasn't replied? Patterns in feedback?
Plan Monday's batch.

## Red lines — do NOT do these

1. **Don't post a cold "I built a halal investing app, please try it!" on a
   subreddit you haven't commented in.** Reddit punishes that fast and bans
   accounts permanently.
2. **Don't email purchased mailing lists.** Reputation kills.
3. **Don't claim a Shariah Supervisory Board until you have one in writing.**
   (The trust-up already removed this; keep the discipline.)
4. **Don't compare Mizan to other apps disparagingly in DMs.** "X app is wrong"
   loses you the people who use X app. "Different angle — implements the rule
   they skip" wins them.
5. **Don't promise paid features or specific dates.** Promises you can't keep
   destroy trust faster than missing features ever will.
6. **Don't reply to a negative DM defensively.** Thank them, fix the thing,
   follow up with the fix. Even one defensive reply gets screenshotted.

## What makes an evangelist

You'll know when it's happening. Signs:

- Someone DMs a friend a Mizan link without you asking, and the friend
  reaches you cold via that link
- Someone posts a screenshot of Mizan on their own X / Instagram / WhatsApp
  story
- Someone writes a long-form post or video about why halal investing apps are
  flawed, and Mizan gets cited
- Someone introduces you to a scholar or community leader on their initiative

**When this happens:**
1. Privately thank them
2. Ship the next thing they suggested faster than they expected
3. When you have 100 users, write a public thank-you post naming the early
   evangelists (only with consent)

  Reddit organic, Show HN, Mufti Faraz Adam outreach). The product premise
  survives contact with users.

  is wrong-for-market and no amount of polish saves it.
- **1-2 shares (in between)** → diagnose: is product wrong, or distribution
  wrong? Test the other variable for 1 more week.
