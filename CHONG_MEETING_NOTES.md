## 4. WHY MIZAN EXISTS (the mission — know this cold)
Islam prohibits **riba** (interest) and profiting from haram activities. So a
Muslim who wants to invest in stocks can't just buy anything — they must check
that a company's business *and* its finances are Shariah-compliant. That's real:
faith over returns.

But here's the problem: **there is no single "halal."** Different scholars and
standards — AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani, S&P DJIM, the Ja'fari (Shia) view, the
strict view — apply different rules and thresholds. **The same stock can be halal
under one and not another.**

Existing apps (Zoya, Musaffa, Wahed, Islamicly) each pick **one** standard
(usually AAOIFI), output a single pass/fail "score," and hide both the
disagreement and the math behind a black box. Worst of all, **none implement
Mufti Taqi Usmani's illiquid-asset rule** — the framework the largest
Hanafi / South-Asian Muslim populations actually follow. So millions are being
quietly mis-served.

**Why this matters to a Muslim personally:** a wrong "halal" label means
unknowingly earning haram income — and in Islam even the *doubtful* is to be
avoided. They want to grow their wealth *without* compromising their faith, and
right now they can't tell whose answer to trust.

**So Mizan exists to remove the doubt:** show every scholar's verdict side by
side, cite every threshold to its primary source, and let the Muslim investor (or
their own scholar) verify the math themselves — calibrated to whichever scholar
*they* follow. Not "trust our score" — "here's the evidence, you decide."

*(One-line mission: "Halal investing, calibrated to your conscience — with the
math shown, not hidden.")*

## 5. WHAT MIZAN DOES (fuller version)
- Type any ticker → instantly see its verdict under **all six methodologies side
  by side**: AAOIFI, Mufti Taqi Usmani, S&P DJIM, Ja'fari (Sistani-aligned),
  Strict/zero-tolerance, and **Custom** (set your own thresholds).
- **Two layers of screening, both shown:**
  1. **Business/sector** — excludes alcohol, gambling, conventional finance
     (riba), tobacco, weapons, pork, adult, non-halal food.
  2. **Financial ratios** — interest-bearing debt / market cap (AAOIFI 30%,
     DJIM 33%), cash + interest-bearing securities / market cap, receivables /
     market cap, impermissible income / revenue (≤5%), and **Mufti Taqi's
     illiquid (tangible) assets ≥ 20% of total assets** — the rule no other app
     implements.
- **Every threshold links to its primary source** — AAOIFI Standard No. 21,
  Mufti Taqi's published rulings, the S&P DJIM manual, Sistani's office, and the
  company's SEC filing. No black-box score; it's auditable.
- **Purification guidance** (dividends only, or dividends + capital gains,
  depending on the scholar).
- Also covers **crypto** (multi-scholar views on BTC, ETH, stablecoins…) and
  **ETFs** (handled correctly — e.g. SPY/QQQ flagged as not Shariah-screened).
- **Free. No signup. No account. No data collected** — your watchlist lives only
  in your browser. (Directly answers the privacy anxiety many feel with finance
  apps.)
- ~340 tickers today, expanding; programmatic pages now make every verdict
  searchable on Google.
