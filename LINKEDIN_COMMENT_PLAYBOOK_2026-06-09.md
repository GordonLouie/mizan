# LinkedIn comment-response playbook — Day 11 post (Version A, live)

Your own notes call babysitting this post the single highest-leverage move of
the week. The first ~12 hours decide whether it lives. Reply to **every**
comment within 30 minutes during that window. Below: a triage rhythm, then
ready-to-paste replies for every comment type you'll realistically get, all
on-message (no emojis, no disparaging other apps, route to hello@, never argue
theology).

The one thing to watch for above all: **anyone tagging a friend or sharing the
post unprompted = an unprompted share.** That's the metric the 30-day exit gate
turns on. Catch it, thank it, log it in the tracker the moment it happens.

---

## Triage rhythm (first 12 hours)

1. Reply within 30 min, in order of signal: shares/tags first, then questions,
   then qualified leads ("I'm in"), then generic praise last.
2. **Public reply for anything others benefit from** (the "how is it different"
   questions) — your answer is content for every lurker.
3. **Move to DM** anything that's a real conversation (a lead, a collaborator,
   a critic with specifics). Reply publicly *once* to acknowledge, then "DMing
   you now."
4. Like every substantive comment. Reply, don't just like, to anything with a
   question mark.
5. Log every commenter who becomes a conversation in `Mizan_Outreach_Tracker.xlsx`.

## Ready replies by comment type

### A. Generic praise — "Nice work" / "Congrats" / "Looks great"
Convert politeness into a behavioral test (the Council's whole point).

> Thanks {name}. If you've got 60 seconds — screen a stock you actually own and
> tell me if the verdict surprised you. That's the feedback that's most useful
> to me right now.

### B. Qualified lead — "I'm in" / "I'll try it" / "DM me"
Acknowledge publicly, take the real ask to DM.

> Appreciate it {name} — DMing you now. Short version: try a stock you own,
> and I'll send three quick questions that take two minutes.

*(Then DM the three tester questions from OUTREACH.md §"Better tester questions".)*

### C. "How's this different from Zoya / Wahed / Musaffa?"
The cleanest, most-reused answer. Keep it to the three points.

> Three things: (1) it shows the verdict under all 6 scholarly methodologies
> side-by-side instead of picking one; (2) it's the only one that implements
> Mufti Taqi Usmani's illiquid-asset rule; (3) every threshold links to its
> primary source — AAOIFI Standard 21, Mufti Taqi's rulings, SEC filings — so
> nothing is a black-box score. Free, no account.

### D. Trust objection — "You're not Muslim, who verifies this?"
The single highest-trust objection. Meet it head-on, humbly. Do not get defensive.

> Fair question, and I'm glad you asked it directly. I'm not Muslim and I'm
> open about that — Mizan doesn't *certify* anything halal. What it does is
> implement each scholar's *published* methodology transparently and cite every
> threshold to its source, so you (or your scholar) can verify the math rather
> than trust my word. A Sharia Supervisory Board review is on the roadmap. Two
> Muslim advisors, including my Founding Advisor Miqdad, keep me honest. If you
> ever spot a ruling I've implemented wrong, that's exactly what I want to hear.

### E. Coverage — "Does it have {ticker} / my market / non-US stocks?"
Honest, and turn the gap into a captured request.

> Right now it's ~340 tickers, US-heavy, and expanding — international coverage
> is improving (I just corrected the data on a batch of foreign-listed names).
> If {ticker} isn't in there yet, tell me and I'll add it — and there's a
> "request a ticker" path on the screen page that routes straight to me.

### F. "My scholar / view isn't one of the six"
Point to the custom builder; stay inclusive.

> The six cover the mainstream standards, but there's a Custom profile where you
> set your own thresholds to match your scholar's exact view — debt cap, income
> cap, illiquid floor, purification scope. If there's a specific published
> methodology you'd want as a named preset, tell me and I'll look at adding it.

### G. Theology / sect debate bait — "halal investing is haram" / school-vs-school
Do not adjudicate. Stay the neutral infrastructure.

> I deliberately don't take a side between the schools — that's not my place.
> Mizan just surfaces what each methodology actually rules, with the sources, so
> people can follow whichever view they hold. Happy to make sure your view is
> represented accurately if you think something's off.

### H. Bug / "this verdict is wrong"
Gold disguised as criticism. Thank, get specifics, fix fast, report back publicly.

> Thank you — this is exactly the kind of catch I need. Which ticker and which
> scholar, and what did you expect to see? If it's a data error I'll fix it fast
> and reply here when it's live. Visible turnaround matters more to me than
> pretending it's perfect.

### I. Collaborator / scholar / dev — "I'd love to help" / "I work in this"
Warm, move to DM, don't over-promise.

> That means a lot {name} — DMing you. Would genuinely value your eyes on it.

### J. Someone shares it / tags a friend → UNPROMPTED SHARE
Highest-value event on the post. Thank privately too, and **log it**.

> Really appreciate you passing it on, {name} — thank you. {To the tagged
> friend:} hello {friend}, happy to answer anything; try a stock you own and
> tell me what's confusing.

> ▸ ACTION: mark this as an unprompted share in `Mizan_Outreach_Tracker.xlsx`.
> This is the metric the 30-day gate depends on. Currently 0 → this would be 1.

- Don't argue theology or sect. Stay the neutral, sourced infrastructure.
